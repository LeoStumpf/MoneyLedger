MoneyLedger is a free, open-source app for sharing expenses in groups — trips, flat shares, dinners — in the spirit of Splitwise or Tricount, but without a MoneyLedger server. Your groups, expenses and payments live in a local database on your devices.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
• Start right away with just your name; groups you don't share never leave your phone
• Share a group with an invite link or QR code: every copy travels end-to-end encrypted over public Nostr relays, which see neither names nor amounts
• Want more security? Connect a Matrix account (matrix.org or your own homeserver) at any time: records are signed by the device that wrote them and travel as end-to-end-encrypted messages; the homeserver only ever sees ciphertext

SPLITTING
• Equal, exact amounts, shares or percentages — always adding up to the cent
• Several payers for one expense, back-dated expenses and payments
• Multiple currencies, with fixed exchange rates per group if you want to settle in one
• Simplified "who pays whom", shareable as text, plus an overview per currency

ORGANISED
• Categories (also your own), notes, search and filters
• Archive settled groups; they come back by themselves when something new is added
• Restore points and "reset this group to an earlier state" undo mistakes
• Optional receipt photo, kept on this device only

YOUR DATA
• Full backup to a file, optionally encrypted with a passphrase, and CSV export/import
• Background sync and notifications about others' changes on Android — no push service, no Firebase
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking

English and German, light and dark theme.
